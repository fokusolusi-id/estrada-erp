A. NEEDS HEAD PDMS APPROVAL (the workbook itself says these are proposals)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1. Sheet 07: the whole authority matrix is a proposal. The job descriptions have no
    "Wewenang" section. Is the matrix approved as written?
A2. Sheet 04 section D, warranty period. Four proposals, none backed by a document:
    a. Tickets carry a source: inherited project defect (Delivery Control, project
       budget) or operational fault (ISO, service budget). Decided jointly by Head ISO
       and Head PMO. Approved?
    b. Responsibility for SLA and availability moves on the date MSO accepts, not on
       the BAST date. The gap is a transition period owned by PMO. Approved?
    c. Replacement under warranty goes to the vendor by RMA at zero cost; outside

    d. PMO leaves the project when the last warranty ends, not at BAST. Status Closing
       only after that. Approved?

    - Declaring RFS: Project Manager declares, Head PMO approves.
    - Preparing the BAST: Project Admin.
    - Approving the BAST: Head PMO.
    - Handover to MSO: Project Manager.
    - Declaring project closure: Head PMO.
    - Proposing contract extension: NO OWNER AT ALL. Who owns it? Candidates named in

A4. Delegation of Authority document is missing. No approval thresholds exist for

B. ROLE AND ACCESS MAPPING IN THIS APP
--------------------------------------
B1. "Head PMO" is implemented as: a Default Owner or Division Owner seated at PMO or
    above it (so Head PDMS also qualifies). Is that the right role for the head of PMO?
B2. Project team candidates are every user holding a seat inside PMO (PMO, PMO-PM,
    PMO-DCA). Should the Project Manager be limited to PMO-PM, and Delivery Control and
    Project Admin to PMO-DCA?
B3. Everyone in PDMS (PMO, SCM, ISO, MSO) and BCD reads every project in their
    entities. Sheet 10 (BATAS AKSES, not read yet) mentions filtering per assignment and
    hiding COGS and GP from PDMS. Should reading be narrowed to assigned people?
B4. BAI verification is open to any BCD requestor, default owner or division owner in
    the entity. Should it be narrowed to specific units, for example Sales Ops?
B5. Built in slice 3 with this mapping, to be confirmed: Head MSO / Head ISO = a
    Default Owner or Division Owner seated exactly at MSO / ISO; Staff Operational EOS
    = anyone seated in MSO-EOS. Unlike Head PMO (B1), Head PDMS does NOT qualify as
    Head MSO or Head ISO, so the giver's superior cannot also be the receiver. Correct?
B6. Sheet 07 row 25 gives Head PMO an approval (S) on submitting the handover package.
    The app has no such step: the Project Manager submits straight to MSO, as sheet 04
    gate 5 describes. Should Head PMO approve the package before it goes to MSO?
B7. Sheet 07 row 26 gives ISO a verification (V) before Head MSO accepts. Not built:
    ISO only acts after MSO accepts. Needed?


C. PROJECT AND SITE RULES
-------------------------
C1. Project status Pengadaan_Barang belongs to SCM (sheet 03, out of scope). Today a
    project jumps from Initiation to Implementasi when its first site is past survey.
    Who sets Pengadaan_Barang, and when?
C2. A site cannot be deleted, only edited. Is deletion needed, and by whom?
C3. "Aktual selesai" on a site is typed by hand. When Assurance verifies the BAI and
    the date is still empty, the app now fills it with the BAI date, so a finished site
    stops counting as late. Is the BAI date the right one, or the date installation
    finished?
C4. Uji petik: the sample flag is set on the site by the Project Manager or Project
    Admin, and the Project Admin confirms "lulus" when compiling the BAI. Is that the
    right person, and is a pass/fail enough or is a document needed?
C5. A returned BAI goes back to status Uji and is collected again from scratch. Should
    it instead return to the previous step only?
C6. Jumlah lokasi kontrak is typed by BCD when the project is opened and cannot be
    changed afterwards. Who may change it (change order), and with whose approval?
C7. BARD and BAUT for TELKOM-scheme customers (sheet 02 step 25) are not built. Needed
    before BAST for those customers?
C8. Planning-stage records are not built: kick-off MoM, Kick-Off Gate, Project Charter,
    Kurva S, Risk Register, baseline approval. In scope for this module?

E. HANDOVER (SLICE 3) AND WARRANTY (SLICE 4)
--------------------------------------------
E1. Credentials and remote access (artefact 7): store only a reference to where the
    credential is kept (vault, owner), or the secret itself? Recommendation: reference
    only. The catch is that the ERP then cannot prove the credential works.
E2. Devices, spares and licences have no register in this app (no warehouse module).
    Proposal: accept them as handover documents first, and build real registers with
    the SCM module. Acceptable for the MSO criteria and the EOS checklist?
E3. The 15 artefacts are built as text references (where the file is kept), following
    the decision for BAI evidence. Confirm, or is upload wanted here?
E4. "Wajib bila ada aplikasi" (artefact 8): built as a tick box any project team
    member sets while drafting the package. Should it come from the contract instead?
E7. The project moves to status Layanan the moment Head MSO accepts, and to Garansi
    once ISO has accepted AND the warranty end date is set. The package status "Selesai"
    is set when the project is closed. Are these the intended meanings?
E8. Head ISO cannot return the package. Sheet 04 gate 8 says a failure escalates to
    Head PDMS. How should that escalation be recorded?
E9. Once Head MSO has accepted, the artefact references are frozen. Who may correct
    one afterwards (for example a moved folder)?
E10. The EOS checklist can be filled as soon as the package is submitted, before Head
    MSO accepts, although sheet 04 orders it after (gate 7). Acceptable?
E5. Warranty end date is built as ONE date per project ("the last warranty"), entered
    by BCD (Kamus Field domain SALES_ASSURANCE). Per-device warranty dates (artefact
    15) are only a handover document. Is one date enough, and is BCD the right owner?
E11. Slice 4 is built on the four section D proposals (A2) BEFORE Head PDMS approved
    them. If any is rejected, the rule and its screen must change.
E12. Inherited defects are a small register on the project, not tickets: reported by
    ISO or MSO, classified jointly by Head ISO and Head PMO, repaired by Delivery
    Control, closed by Head PMO. When the ticket module (sheet 05) is built, should
    this register be replaced by the ticket's "sumber_gangguan" field?
E19. One head alone can set a report aside as operational (not the reporter). Sheet
    07 says inherited-or-not is decided jointly. Should "operational" also need both
    heads, like "inherited" does?
E13. A report that neither head has acted on blocks closing the project. Should there
    be a time limit, or an escalation to Head PDMS?
E14. Sheet 07 row 32 has the Project Manager verify (V) the corrective work before
    Head PMO closes it. Not built: Delivery Control records, Head PMO closes. Needed?
E15. Who pays (A2c: RMA at zero cost under warranty, service budget outside it) is not
    recorded anywhere yet. It needs device warranty dates and a budget link.
E16. Closing is allowed from the warranty end date itself (that day counts as ended).
    Correct, or the day after?
E17. Project status Drop cannot be set from any screen. Who may drop a project?
E18. Proposing a contract extension (A3) is not built: it has no owner.
E6. service_agreement_id is meant to be filled when the BAST is approved. Service
    agreements do not exist in this app. Out of scope until the ticket module?

H. BUILT IN THE SECOND PASS (14 Nov 2026): DEFAULTS CHOSEN, TO CONFIRM
----------------------------------------------------------------------
These close earlier questions (C2, C6, C7, C8, D2, E15, E17, F1) with a default. None
has been approved by the client.

H1. Emails (F1). One per handoff, never per site, no rupiah, never to the person who
    did it. Sent for: team assigned, RFS declared, BAST prepared, BAST approved (to
    BCD and the Project Manager), handover submitted, handover returned, handover
    accepted by MSO (to Head ISO and EOS), fault reported (Head ISO and Head PMO),
    defect confirmed inherited (Delivery Control). NOT sent: per-site BAI steps (a
    206-site project would send 206 emails per step), site edits, contract changes,
    warranty date. Should the per-site BAI steps get a daily digest instead?
H2. Site deletion (C2). The Project Manager deletes a site only while it is Belum
    Mulai or Survei, has no BAI, and the project is still before Penagihan. Right
    person and right limit?
H3. Contract site count (C6). BCD changes it, with a written reason, until the BAST
    is approved; never below the sites already registered. There is NO approval step
    and no value threshold: the Delegation of Authority (A4) does not exist. Should a
    change order need the Head PMO's approval, or the customer's addendum number?
H4. KPI (D2). Targets are the job descriptions' own: Delivery Control 7 days from the
    BAI date to recording it, Project Admin 5 days from recording to compiling,
    Assurance 3 days from compiling to verifying. Each step is counted from the one
    before, NOT from "installation finished" as KPI 3 and KPI 1 say, because the app
    does not record when installation finished. The conflict in sheet 02 section C
    (D1) is therefore avoided, not resolved. Which clock does the client want?
    Business days or calendar days? The report is per project; a per-person, per-month
    report is not built.
H5. Status Drop (E17). The Head PMO drops a project with a reason, only before the
    BAST is approved. Dropped and closed projects are read-only. Should BCD (who owns
    the contract) have to agree? Can a dropped project be reopened?
H6. BARD and BAUT (C7). A tick box "skema TELKOM" set by BCD, then Assurance (BCD)
    records the number and date of each document after RFS is approved; the BAST is
    held until both exist. Is a TELKOM customer known from the contract or the deal
    (so the box could be filled automatically)? Who signs BARD and BAUT, and are two
    numbers and dates enough?
H7. Planning records (C8). Five records (kick-off notes, MoM, charter, risk register,
    requirements), each a text reference to where the file is kept, like BAI
    evidence. Kick-Off Gate needs the kick-off and MoM; the baseline needs the gate,
    charter, risk register, a complete team and a target RFS date. Both are approved
    by a Head PMO who is NOT on the project team. Field work (Staging, Dikirim,
    Instalasi, Uji) is blocked until the baseline is approved; survey is allowed.
    Kurva S is computed from the sites' planned and actual finish dates. Open points:
      - Requirements (record 5) is not required by any gate. Should it be?
      - Charter holds a reference only, not the budget or timeline themselves.
      - After the baseline, a change of a site's planned date is NOT yet a change
        order: it is just audited. Who approves one?
      - Existing projects without a baseline cannot move a site into Staging until a
        Head PMO approves one.
H8. Who pays (E15). Shown on each fault once classified: inherited defect = project
    budget, with an RMA claim where the device is under warranty; operational fault =
    service budget with Head ISO approval. No amount, no RMA number and no per-device
    warranty date is recorded (there is no device register: sheet 03 / SCM). Is the
    label enough for now?
H9. Not clicked through in a browser (see G2), added this pass: delete site, change
    contract count, drop, TELKOM documents, planning page and Kurva S, and the
    emails. Rules are unit tested; actions and email delivery are not exercised.
